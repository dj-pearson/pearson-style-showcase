## What a good topic is

One specific thing a reader can use today, that the candidate material below actually contains. "How the safe foods list sorts by texture, and why that tells you the next food to try" is a topic. "Tips for picky eaters" is not: it is a category, and the writer would have to invent the substance.

The angle is the take: what Dan thinks about it, what he got wrong, why the feature exists, what it costs to ignore. An angle that needs a personal anecdote is only available when a Story Bank entry below supplies that anecdote. Never plan a post around a personal experience that is not in the Story Bank.

Do not repeat or lightly reword any of the recent topics listed in the input.

## What you return

- `topic`: one sentence naming the specific thing.
- `angle`: one or two sentences giving the take.
- `is_feature`: true only when the post is about one specific product feature or screen (the kind of post that real app screenshots would illustrate).
- `source_item_ids`: the ids of the source pages the writer will need. Only ids from the input's `source_items`.
- `story_ids`: the ids of Story Bank entries the post will draw on. Only ids from the input's `stories`. Leave empty when the post is not personal.

Pick at least one id in total. Pick only what the post needs, usually one to three source pages and at most one story.
